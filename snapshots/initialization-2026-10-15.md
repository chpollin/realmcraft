# Initialization Chat Prototype

The owner authorized the facilitator to select a fitting starting concept, set up the chat game and continue observation. This snapshot records the proposed starting state before the first player action. It is a development simulation, with no kernel campaign created and no German audio generated.

## Owner inputs

Magic and technology, interest in data and orchestration, an original world, learning about worldbuilding, concrete selectable offers and an editable draft. Narration is calm and mysterious with some dry humour. The owner delegated the starting choice after inspecting the formats, without explicitly selecting A, B or C.

## Facilitator defaults for this run

- Working title. Die offenen Werkstaetten, rendered as Die offenen Werkstätten in the player text.
- Basis. Collaboration with intelligences, chosen for its connection to the stated interest in data and orchestration.
- Community. Several households share a workshop and its records in a riverside neighbourhood. This is a starting community, with the larger people and world still open.
- Player role. Coordinator of the workshop. Collaborators retain their own judgment.
- Provisional appearance. Daylight, worn tools, glazed ceramics, water and restrained blue-green accents. Visual approval is open.
- Beliefs. Inhabitants can differ about the nature of the intelligences. No actual deity or comprehensive religion is established.
- Fictional ability. A machine can imprint descriptions derived from material measurements into a receptive manufactured substance, changing its shape or permeability. The process needs matter and energy, works locally and can fail when descriptions omit relevant conditions. Agreement among intelligences alone does not make a description correct.
- Collaborators. One intelligence compares records and evidence; another proposes designs from the available description. Both can be mistaken. The player can ask for sources, compare answers, request a small test or choose another approach.
- Resolution. Qualitative narrated consequences in this prototype. No probability, resource count or new executable kernel mechanic is claimed.

## Opening situation

The workshop is asked to repair the membrane of a shared water filter. Records say the last design passed a test, but the installed membrane now blocks water. A resident brings the faulty piece. The first task is to understand the failure and decide how to attempt repair.

Offered approaches are checking records and material samples, requesting and comparing the intelligences' explanations, speaking to the people who use the filter, or taking a free action. Before a consequential experiment, state its intended effect, known uncertainty and foreseeable cost in the fiction. Revisions to setup remain available.

## Observation cutoff

A separate observer reviewed selected conversation steps and the delegated-start brief. It distinguished delegated facilitator choices from confirmed player preferences. The player has not yet made an in-world decision, so engagement, comprehension and a successful game start remain unassessed.

The full evaluation and durable integration follow the player's first actions and feedback. The maintained method is in [new-game-design.md](../knowledge/new-game-design.md).
